### Researcher, Stream Data Analytics and Machine Learning Lab

**Novosibirsk State University**, Russia  
**Nov 2025 – Sep 2026**

- Built **ClaimGraph-BT**, a pipeline that detects scientific claims in 1,585 brain tumor AI papers (17,304 sentences) using fine-tuned PubMedBERT and BioBERT, extracts structured fields with Flan-T5, and links claims across papers with SPECTER2 clustering and graph layers. The best model (PubMedBERT) reached 0.84 accuracy and 0.93 AUC.
- Co-authored **MedSETNER**, a benchmark corpus of 691 medical articles with 9,376 annotated dataset-name mentions (Fleiss' κ = 0.85); contributed to dataset annotation.
- Coordinated lab projects in machine learning and data analytics.
