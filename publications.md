## Conference Papers

- **Hussain, N.**, Haider, T., Pavlovskiy, E. N. [*ClaimGraph-BT: Automated Claim Extraction and Cross-Paper Comparison for Brain Tumor AI Literature*](https://aistconf.org/program/accepted/). International Conference on Analysis of Images, Social Networks and Texts (**AIST 2026**), Oct. 2026. Oral presentation.  
  A pipeline that detects scientific claims in 1,585 brain tumor AI papers, extracts structured fields and links claims across papers.

- Haider, T., **Hussain, N.**, Zaidi, S. M. I., Pavlovskiy, E. N. [*MedSETNER: A Benchmark Corpus for Extracting Dataset Names from Medical Scientific Literature*](https://aistconf.org/program/accepted/). International Conference on Analysis of Images, Social Networks and Texts (**AIST 2026**), Oct. 2026. Oral presentation.  
  A benchmark of 691 medical articles with 9,376 annotated dataset-name mentions.

- **Hussain, N.** [*AI-Assisted Scientific Claim Extraction and Evidence Comparison in Brain Tumor Research*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tddST7kzO0r70AtpnmkIwMR1-JXzPGmF/view?usp=sharing). International Conference on Higher Education and Sustainable Development (**ICHESD 2026**), Sep. 2026. Oral presentation.

- **Hussain, N.** [*Automatic Identification of Main Scientific Claims in Brain Tumor Research*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ggrF4KO0omkfa0SNTijafr8om75m4xqt/view?usp=sharing). International Scientific Student Conference (**MNSK 2026**), Novosibirsk, Russia, Apr. 2026. Oral presentation.
